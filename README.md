## Who we are

**MountainTech+** is an engineering and technology company founded in **2021** in Bursa, Türkiye. We build AI-powered digital and industrial systems where off-the-shelf software stops fitting: custom software, ERP and marketplace integrations, AI agents, and vision-based quality control.

We don't leave AI at the demo stage. We map the real process first, build on top of it, agree on metrics before go-live, and hand over a system your team can own.
